From the [SAG page](https://members.sagfoundation.org/programs/19109): There are more opportunities in promo VO for actors of all voice prints and backgrounds than ever. But the subtleties and nuances of working reads require ever more from us as actors. The primary focus of this class will be to get students into the booth to receive feedback and direction. Marie and Jack also will briefly examine current trends, examples, and strategies for developing your read and finding work in VO promo.
